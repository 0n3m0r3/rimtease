## Shared documentation resources

These resources belong to the documentation. Keep one source and link to it from each relevant mod instead of creating another commission.

### Explanatory diagrams

<span class="rt-label">Optional editorial need</span>

Potential subjects include the roles of the mods and the distinction between anatomy, function, individual parts, clothing coverage and appearance. Existing text or tables may be sufficient.

The documentation owns the illustration, with review by the relevant mod. Choose the actual explanation before drawing; use original or authorized resources and label proposed behaviour clearly. An explanatory diagram does not replace Body's in-game medical atlas.

### Guide screenshots

<span class="rt-label">Optional editorial need · Capture after verification</span>

Potential examples include Body's size settings, surgical choices, medical diagram and preparation for removal, or Themes' rendering settings and comparisons. Reuse a suitable validation capture when it answers the same question.

Capture released or explicitly identified preview interfaces with their game, mod and DLC versions. Installation screenshots require a real distribution. A mockup is not a working-game screenshot. Add useful captions and text alternatives before publication.
